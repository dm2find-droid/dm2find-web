## Who we are and what this policy covers

DM2find BV ("DM2find", "we") builds an AI travel concierge for tourism
boards and destination marketing organisations ("destinations") and for
the visitors who use it. We are based in Hilversum, the Netherlands
(Chamber of Commerce number 89552407). You can reach us at
sales@dm2find.ai.

This policy explains what personal data we process, why, who receives
it, and what rights you have. It covers the dm2find.ai website, the chat
assistant on destination websites, in the full-screen chat at
chat.dm2find.ai and in embedded widgets, voice conversations, dictation,
and any messaging channel a destination switches on.

Our role depends on where you are. When you talk to a destination's
assistant, the destination decides why and how your conversation is used
and is normally the data controller. DM2find processes the data on its
behalf as a processor. For our own website, demos and sales
conversations, DM2find is the controller. A destination's name and logo
in a demo show which place the assistant covers; they do not mean the
destination is our customer.

You are talking to an AI. Its answers can be incomplete or wrong, so
check opening hours, prices and bookings with the provider before you
rely on them.

following, depending on how you use it.

them.

## How voice and dictation work

The microphone is only used after you tap a button, and your browser
asks for permission first. You can stop at any time. A live voice
conversation ends automatically after five minutes.

**Browser dictation.** On some destinations the microphone button in the
text box uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. In Chrome, for
example, the audio is processed by Google under Google's own privacy
policy. DM2find does not control or receive that audio.

**Voice assistant (standard).** For destinations with a live voice
assistant, your audio is streamed to ElevenLabs, which turns your speech
into text and generates the spoken reply. Audio recording is switched
off at ElevenLabs, so the audio itself is not kept. The transcript
appears in the chat so you can read along. ElevenLabs processes this
data in the United States.

**EU-only option.** Destinations can choose a fully European setup.
Dictation audio is then recorded in your browser, converted to a short
audio file and sent through our own servers in Germany to a
speech-to-text service hosted by Scaleway in the EU. The European voice
assistant and the European chat use open models for listening and
speaking and Mistral for the answers, all hosted by Scaleway in France
and the Netherlands, with the destination data search on our own servers
in Germany. No US provider is involved.

DM2find does not store dictation audio. The workflow that handles it is
set not to keep the audio or the resulting text after a successful
request. Transcripts of voice conversations are anonymous and are kept
only for as long as they are useful to improve answer quality, and no
longer than 12 months.

## Messaging channels

A destination can offer the assistant on WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger,
Instagram Direct, Telegram, WeChat or LINE. In production this always
runs on the destination's own account on that platform. The destination
is then the controller, and DM2find processes your messages on its
behalf.

**What we receive.** The messages you send, the platform's identifier
for you (on WhatsApp this is your phone number), your profile name if
the platform shares it, your language and the time.

**What the platform does.** The platform delivers your messages and is a
separate controller of its own processing, under its own terms and
privacy policy: Meta for WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger and Instagram,
Telegram for Telegram, Tencent for WeChat and LY Corporation for LINE.
These platforms may process data outside the EU, and we do not control
that.

**How we use it.** To answer you, as described in this policy. We do not
use your details from a messaging channel for advertising.

**Your choices.** You can stop messaging at any time, and block or
delete the chat in the app. Deleting it in the app does not delete it
from our systems, so ask the destination or us if you want your
conversation removed.

## Why we process data and on what basis

We use personal data only for the purposes below. Where a destination is
the controller, it determines the legal basis; the bases listed are the
ones that normally apply.

| Purpose                                                | Legal basis (GDPR)                                                                        |
|--------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Answer your questions and run the assistant            | Legitimate interests in providing the service you asked for, or performance of a contract |
| Use of the microphone for voice and dictation          | Your consent, given when you tap the button and allow microphone access in your browser   |
| Security, abuse prevention and fixing errors           | Legitimate interests in keeping the service safe and working                              |
| Improving answer quality and the service               | Legitimate interests, using as little personal data as possible                           |
| Responding to enquiries, demos and sales conversations | Legitimate interests, or steps taken at your request before a contract                    |
| Meeting legal and accounting duties                    | Legal obligation                                                                          |

You can withdraw consent at any time by ending the conversation or
blocking microphone access in your browser. That does not affect
processing that already took place. We do not use your conversations for
advertising, and we do not use them to make decisions that have legal or
similarly significant effects on you.

receive your name, email address and chosen time and handle them under
their own privacy policies. We then see the same details in our
calendar.

## Transfers outside the EU

Our servers are in Germany. Destinations that choose the EU-only option
keep voice, dictation and chat processing inside the EU.

On the standard setup, some providers process data in the United States:
ElevenLabs for voice, OpenAI for chat answers, and your browser vendor
if you use browser dictation. We rely on the EU-US Data Privacy
Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the European
Commission's standard contractual clauses, together with the safeguards
described in the provider's terms. You can ask us for a copy of the
safeguards that apply. Messaging platforms, Cal.com and Google process
data under their own terms and may do so outside the EU.

## How long we keep data

We keep personal data no longer than needed for the purpose it was
collected for. The periods below are the ones we apply.

| Data                                     | Retention                                                                                                      |
|------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Dictation audio                          | Not stored by DM2find                                                                                          |
| Live voice audio                         | Not stored by DM2find. Audio recording is switched off at ElevenLabs.                                          |
| Chat conversations and voice transcripts | As long as they are useful to improve answer quality, and no longer than 12 months, then deleted or anonymised |
| Technical and server logs                | Up to 7 days                                                                                                   |
| Emails you send us                       | Until the matter is resolved                                                                                   |
| Accounting records                       | As long as the law requires                                                                                    |

If a destination is the controller, it can set a shorter period for its
own assistant.

## Cookies and local storage

The assistant stores a small amount of data in your browser, only to
make it work: a random identifier that keeps your conversation together,
and a saved copy of the destination's colours and styling so the page
loads quickly. This is strictly necessary for the service and is not
used for tracking or advertising.

The dm2find.ai website does not currently use analytics. If we add
cookies that need your consent, we will ask for it first. You can clear
stored data at any time in your browser settings.

## Your rights, security and contact

**Your rights.** Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to your data,
correction, deletion, restriction of processing and a portable copy. You
can object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw
consent at any time. We answer within one month. If your conversation
took place on a destination's assistant, we may pass your request to
that destination, which is normally the controller. To help us find your
conversation, tell us roughly when and where you used the assistant.

**Security.** We use encrypted connections, access controls, European
hosting and logical separation between destinations. We keep access to
personal data to the people and systems that need it. No online service
is completely secure, so please do not share sensitive details in the
chat.

**Children.** The assistant is not aimed at children under 16, and we do
not knowingly collect their data.

**Complaints.** You can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority
(Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or to the authority in your own country.
